Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data Calvaire Group LLC collects through calvairegroup.com and when you work with us, why we use it, and the rights you have over it.

Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Calvaire Group LLC, 30 N Gould St, Ste R, Sheridan, WY 82801, USA. For any privacy question or request, email info@calvairegroup.com.

What we collect

  • What you send us by email: your name, email address, company, and anything you include in your message, such as product details or links to your ads.
  • Client materials: if you become a client, the product images, brand assets, scripts and footage you share so we can produce your videos.
  • Technical data: when you visit the site, our hosting provider automatically processes your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to deliver the site and keep it secure.

We do not use analytics, advertising pixels or tracking tools on this site, and we do not sell personal data.

Why we use it and on what legal basis

  • To answer your enquiry and prepare a proposal, based on steps you ask us to take before entering a contract.
  • To deliver our services and invoice them, based on our contract with you.
  • To keep the site running and secure, based on our legitimate interest in operating a reliable website.
  • To meet legal obligations, such as keeping accounting and tax records.

Who we share it with

We only share data with service providers we need to run the business, under agreements that protect it:

  • GitHub, Inc. (website hosting)
  • Tutao GmbH, Tuta Mail (email)
  • AI video and image generation tools, only for client materials needed to produce your videos
  • Payment processors and our accountants, when you become a client

We may also disclose data where the law requires it.

International transfers

Calvaire Group LLC is based in the United States and some of our providers process data outside the European Economic Area. Where we transfer personal data from the EEA or UK, we rely on adequacy decisions, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don't lead to a project: up to 12 months after our last contact.
  • Client data and project files: for the length of the project and up to 3 years after, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
  • Invoices and accounting records: as long as tax law requires.
  • Hosting logs: kept by our hosting provider for a short period for security.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to withdraw any consent you gave. Email info@calvairegroup.com and we will reply within 30 days. If you are in the EEA or UK and think we have not handled your data properly, you can complain to your local data protection authority, such as the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos in Spain.

Cookies

This site does not set cookies and does not use tracking technologies. Fonts are hosted on our own domain, so no data is sent to third-party font services.

Children

Our services are for businesses. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
